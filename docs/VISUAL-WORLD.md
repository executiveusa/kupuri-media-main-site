## POSITION (from 10-agency gauntlet: NoGood, MuteSix, KlientBoost, Disruptive, Tinuiti, Media.Monks MX, Performly, EPA Digital, Brand Industry, Hogarth MX)

PROVEN: performance-creative strategy (hooks, UGC, ad variations, testing velocity) - validated by all ten benchmark agencies.

None of the ten holds this combination. Imitation risk: low - the two-country, women-led, AI-native craft position cannot be claimed honestly by a network or a US-only shop.

## TERRITORY A - "RESTRAINED": THE ATELIER
Governing idea: a serious studio. Swiss grid, generous whitespace, evidence-led pages.
Grid: 12-col, strict. Type: single grotesque family, two weights. Color: near-black, bone, one accent (terracotta).
Signature moment: case-study numbers revealed on scroll - proof as the only decoration.
Imitation risk: medium (looks like every good boutique) - mitigated by content, not form.
Usability ceiling: highest. Originality ceiling: lowest.

## TERRITORY B - "EXPRESSIVE" (RECOMMENDED): CHAQUIRA
Governing idea: bead by bead. Huichol chaquira craft as the operating metaphor - strategy assembled piece by piece, two cultures woven into one pattern, patience + precision.
Grid: 12-col broken by bead-cluster motifs at section seams. Type: display serif with hand-cut feel + grotesque body. Color: the bead palette of the mark on deep charcoal; gradients only where the mark colors meet.
Signature moment: the Kupuri bead intro (12s, already rendered from the real mark) as the site entrance - every section transition resolves like beads locking into pattern.
Imitation risk: low - no competitor can borrow the metaphor without owning this mark and this story.
Usability: must pass Krug gate (intro skippable, <3s to content, reduced-motion variant).

## TERRITORY C - "EXPERIMENTAL": THE BORDER NEWSROOM
Governing idea: a living binational feed - the site as a working desk: live campaign fragments, hooks in testing, both currencies, both languages side by side.
Grid: modular stream. Type: mono accents + editorial sans. Color: newsprint + signal red.
Signature moment: real ad variations being tested, visible in situ.
Imitation risk: very low. Usability risk: high (novel layout, Krug gate likely fails first pass) - kept as provocation, not candidate.
